# VOICE.md
Brand voice for FRAIMOUS — how the house sounds, words we use, words we never use. 2026-08-17.

## How we sound

Collector. Dry. Short.

We write like a small house that already knows who it is for. We do not explain the business. We do not sell a process. The still is the sentence.

Quiet like LNDN Gray. Scarce like House of Spoils. One line, then stop.

## The house

FRAIMOUS is an image house. We curate and iterate the best digital-born stills, then frame them. Editorial. Not a shop. Not logistics.

Name is **FRAIMOUS**. Never Frameous. Never Fraimeus.

## How we write

- Short sentences. Periods, not hype.
- Piece titles stand alone. Do not narrate a plot.
- Price is a floor, not a sale. Never “starting at” with cheer.
- Inquire is the only CTA.
- Public email: hello@fraimous.com
- Do not mention domains, Vercel, Printful, Shopify, Grok, or the studio floor in public copy.
