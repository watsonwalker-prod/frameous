# PACK-01 — Capsule Domestic

**Lane:** narrative tableau · extraordinary ordinary  
**When to use:** Quiet interiors. A woman alone with coffee, a broadsheet, a robe or flight suit, and a porthole that does not look at Earth. This is Drop 01’s strongest recurring scene — *American Newspaper Capsule*, *#7 Crop Top*, *Desert Sunrise Coffee*. Open this pack when you want a still that reads as a film beat, not a lookbook.

Grounded in: `images/02_newspaper.jpg`, `images/04_seven.jpg`, `images/05_desert.jpg` · inbox `frm_035`.

---

## Locked style bible

| Axis | Lock |
|------|------|
| **Light** | One hard directional key (camera-left or from the porthole). Rembrandt on any visible face. Long, readable shadows on metal or wood. No fill that flattens. Slight highlight bloom / Pro-Mist, not glow-slop. |
| **Lens** | 35mm or 50mm, f/2–2.8. Vertical **2:3** or **4:5**. Medium-long or three-quarter. Subject sharp; porthole and cabin ribs fall off. |
| **Palette** | Charcoal, brushed silver, cream, warm wood, one white ceramic. Accents: maroon **7**, eagle-patch gold, ochre desert *only through glass*. Overall grade: warm analog, slightly desaturated. |
| **Wardrobe / set** | Satin robe (dark or silver) *or* cream canvas flight suit *or* white structured crop + matching trousers. House motifs allowed: circular eagle / globe patch, word AMERICAN or UNITED STATES as *embroidered patch only*, large numeral **7**. White ceramic cup + saucer. Vintage broadsheet. Fluffy white slippers *or* bare feet on wood/grate. Cabin: curved metal ribs, pill or rounded-rect porthole, grate or warm plank floor, a few analog switches — never a glass-cockpit HUD. Optional: ornate carved desk, small ceramic dog figurine. Mid-century bob or soft 60s set. |
| **Refuse** | Neon cockpit LEDs, NASA meatball / real agency marks, readable newspaper masthead of a real paper, celebrity face, smile-to-camera, plastic skin, extra fingers, white seamless studio, spacesuit helmet, lensflare pack, CGI chrome, text in the image except the patch/numeral. |

**House finish:** fine 35mm film grain (5219 / Portra 400 density). Tactile fabric. Collector still, not a still from a video game.

## Ready-to-paste prompts

### 01-A — Newspaper, robe, star void
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, 50mm f/2. A woman seated three-quarter from behind in a retro-futuristic cabin, mid-century brown bob, dark glossy satin robe with a large embroidered circular patch on the back: eagle, the word AMERICAN, maroon numeral 7. Oversized fluffy white slippers. She reads a vintage broadsheet at a small white pedestal table; one white ceramic cup and saucer. Behind her a large pill-shaped porthole opens onto a deep black starfield. Curved brushed-metal walls, analog switch panel above the window, metal grate floor. Hard warm key from camera-right, Rembrandt falloff, sharp shadow of head and paper on the cabin wall. Fine analog film grain, tactile satin, no glow slop. FRAIMOUS still.

### 01-B — #7 kneel, flag mug, wood capsule
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, 35mm f/2.8. A woman kneeling in profile on warm polished wood-plank flooring inside a 1970s spacecraft cabin. Wavy brown hair, looking away. White sleeveless structured crop with shoulder pads and a sequined numeral 7 on the chest; small flag patch on the shoulder; matching tight white trousers. In front of her a white ceramic mug with a quiet flag decal, visible steam. Curved metallic structural ribs; large window to a black starfield. Golden-hour raking light, long sharp shadows, soft bloom on highlights, Kodak-warm grade, fine grain. Solitary, expensive, not a costume shoot.

### 01-C — Desert sunrise, flight suit, carved desk
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, 50mm. Woman seen from the back, seated at an ornate dark-wood carved desk, 1960s dark bob with a thin headband, holding a white ceramic cup to her lips. Heavy cream cotton flight suit; large circular embroidered patch on the back — UNITED STATES around an eagle and globe — and a dark numeral 7 below. On the desk a small stylized ceramic Shiba figurine. Behind the desk a rounded-rectangular porthole: hazy ochre-orange desert planet, pale low sun, atmospheric bloom. Soft directional window light, nostalgic 1970s sci-fi still, analog grain, muted earth palette. Quiet, not heroic.

### 01-D — Silver robe, hard cabin shadow
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3. Same capsule language as 01-A but cooler metal. Woman in a shimmering silver satin robe, AMERICAN eagle + 7 patch, face hidden behind an open broadsheet. White cup. Pill window to crisp white stars on black. Hard key from front-left; the shadow of her head and the paper prints cleanly on the curved metal. Vintage aeronautical dials above the glass. Fine grain, high contrast, mid-century domestic in a void. No readable masthead.

### 01-E — Table still (no face)
Editorial still life, vertical 4:5, 50mm. Empty cabin seat edge in soft focus. White pedestal table: folded vintage broadsheet, white cup and saucer, thin ribbon of steam, one dark dried stem in a tiny black vessel. Porthole behind as a dark oval of stars, out of focus. Warm side light, long shadow of the cup. Analog grain. Object as talisman — no figure, no logo type.

### 01-F — Night cabin, blue-black void
Same identity and robe as 01-A. Lights down. Key is a single practical from the switch panel, cooler. Porthole is blue-black void with sparse stars, no golden hour. She holds the cup with both hands, newspaper closed on the table. Grain heavier, shadows deeper. Same cabin geometry. Hold the patch and the 7.

### 01-G — Profile drink, cream canvas, ochre glass
Close three-quarter profile, vertical 4:5. Cream canvas flight suit collar and the edge of the back patch only. Cup at the lips. Porthole fills the background with ochre haze and a pale coin of sun. Soft bloom, fine grain, almost no cabin chrome. Contemplative. No eye contact.

### 01-H — Two places at the table
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3. Same wood-floor capsule as 01-B. One figure in the white #7 crop, seated on the floor, back three-quarter. Two white cups — one steaming, one empty. Starfield window. Golden raking light. Suggest a departed other without showing them. Solitary, not sad-stock.
