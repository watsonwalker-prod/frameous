# PACK-04 — Archer, Gilded Hall

**Lane:** tactical editorial · architecture shadow  
**When to use:** A bow as a piece of sculpture, an olive jumpsuit as wardrobe, a carved gold interior as the other character. This is *Archer* and the cockpit cousin `frm_057`. Open when you want stoic fashion-meets-form, Rembrandt face, ornate stone — not action-movie aim.

Grounded in: `images/03_archer.jpg`.

---

## Locked style bible

| Axis | Lock |
|------|------|
| **Light** | Low-key Rembrandt. Warm edge on cheekbone, bow limb, and gold carving. Background falls to shadow. In the cockpit cousin: harder editorial key, still not on-camera flash trash. |
| **Lens** | 50mm f/2–2.8, vertical 2:3. Three-quarter or medium. Shallow DOF — pillar and window soften. |
| **Palette** | Olive drab / taupe jumpsuit, charcoal, warm gold leaf, deep brown wood of the bow, amber window. Earth, not camo print. |
| **Wardrobe / set** | Form-fitting high-neck tactical / flight jumpsuit, muted olive. Hair in a tight sleek bun. Matte-black *or* traditional wood recurve — elegant, not compound-bow gadgetry. Optional small flag or 7 patch on the arm, quiet. Set A: high ornate hall, carved pillar with gold, tall window. Set B: cramped analog cockpit, switches, circular porthole to black void, grated floor, harness. One set per prompt. |
| **Refuse** | Compound bows, quivers as mall cosplay, camo pattern, plate carrier mall-ninja, aiming at camera, war-face, blood, medieval princess, elf, white wedding hall, neon cockpit, readable patch paragraphs. |

**Tone:** stoic, nocturnal, expensive. The bow is form. The hall is the other actor.

## Ready-to-paste prompts

### 04-A — Draw in the gilded hall (hero)
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, 50mm f/2.8. Woman in three-quarter profile, dark hair in a tight sleek bun, determined focus not a snarl. High-neck form-fitting olive-drab jumpsuit, small quiet patch on the upper arm. She draws a large matte-black recurve, arrow back, leading hand on the grip. Behind her a carved ornate pillar with gold metallic accents; high-ceiling hall, soft window light far back, shallow DOF. Low-key Rembrandt on the face and the bow’s edge. Warm golds, deep shadow, analog grain. FRAIMOUS still.

### 04-B — Cockpit archer
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3. Same bun and olive flight suit, now with a black harness and a few worn patches (small flag, circular mission patch, numeral 7 — no paragraphs of type). Traditional wooden recurve held diagonally, one extra arrow in the off hand, white-and-red fletching. Cramped analog cockpit: switches, red and white toggles, pipes, grated floor. Circular porthole of black void behind her head. Hard directional editorial key, deep shadows, used-future tactility, fine grain. Cool, not cute.

### 04-C — Hall without her
Architecture still, vertical 2:3, 35mm. The carved gold pillar and the empty hall from 04-A. Tall window, mullion shadow thrown across stone. Dust in the beam. No figure, no bow. Low-key, warm stone, analog grain. Architecture shadow lane.

### 04-D — Bow as object
Still life, vertical 4:5, 85mm. Matte-black recurve and one arrow on dark marble, a strip of olive fabric in the corner, gold carving out of focus behind. Side light, long shadow. No hands. Relic.

### 04-E — Leaving the hall
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3. Same woman, bow slung, walking away down the gilded hall. We see bun, olive back, gold pillars receding. Window light from one side. Grain. Solitary.

### 04-F — Profile aim, tighter
4:5 crop of 04-A. Face, bun, the bow limb cutting the frame, one gold capital behind. Rembrandt. Hold identity. Less set, more sculpture.

### 04-G — Olive + cream crossover
Same hall and bow as 04-A, wardrobe swapped to the cream canvas flight suit (back patch not required). Hold face and bun. Grade slightly warmer. Iterate axis: wardrobe only.

### 04-H — Night window, no gold
Same archer, same olive, same draw — but the hall is stripped: charcoal stone, one tall window, no gilt. Cooler grade, deeper blacks. Tests whether the piece holds without ornament.
