# PACK-03 — Grid Figure

**Lane:** editorial fashion still · body in geometric void  
**When to use:** A figure moving through an infinite gold lattice with black ink-smoke and amber uplight. White technical wardrobe as the only cold note. This is *Golden Grid Climber* and inbox `frm_032` / `frm_059`. Open when you want architecture as a cage and the body as the product.

Grounded in: `images/01_golden_grid.jpg`.

---

## Locked style bible

| Axis | Lock |
|------|------|
| **Light** | Low warm key — firelight / under-sun — hitting the underside of rods and the body. High contrast. Deep blacks in the ink. Background haze ochre-gold, not white. |
| **Lens** | 35mm or 50mm, vertical 2:3. Figure sharp; grid recedes to a vanishing point and softens. Slight motion in hair or limbs OK; no smear. |
| **Palette** | Stark white (wardrobe), ink black (smoke), brass / pale-gold rods, burnt orange, ochre, pale yellow. Warm vintage film grade. Optional graphic-sepia cousin (frm_059) — lock one grade per batch. |
| **Wardrobe / set** | Crisp white high-neck long-sleeve bodysuit, subtle seaming. White thigh-high stockings or ruched white boots. Athletic build, hair in motion. Set: thin gold or pale-wood rods in a 3D scaffold / wireframe that recedes forever. Thick opaque black swirls — ink in water or oily smoke — in foreground and mid. No floor you can name. Solitary. |
| **Refuse** | Superhero pose, gym lighting, neon cyber grid, Tron, LED purple, latex shine, logo on the suit, readable type, extra limbs, melted hands, white cyclorama, daylight blue, cute. |

**Kinship:** this is fashion in a void, not sci-fi concept art. If it looks like a game key-art, kill it.

---

## Ready-to-paste prompts

### 03-A — Leap through gold lattice (hero)
Editorial fashion photograph, vertical 2:3, 35mm f/2.8. Athletic woman in a crisp white high-neck long-sleeve bodysuit and white thigh-high stockings, mid-leap / levitating on a diagonal through a three-dimensional scaffold of thin glowing gold rods that recede to a vanishing point. Thick black ink-smoke swirls in the foreground. Warm amber uplight from below, firelight on the rods and the body, deep blacks. Hair in motion. Fine analog grain, painterly-real, 1970s sci-fi editorial not CGI. FRAIMOUS still.

### 03-B — Climb, worm’s-eye, ochre graphic
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, low angle. Same white technical wardrobe. She climbs the lattice, gripping cross-beams; we look up a curving gold/wood grid that tapers into height. Palette shifts toward ochre, clay, sepia, ink-black shadows — lithograph warmth, still photographic. Smoke at the base. Solitary, monumental. Fine grain. No illustration hatching.

### 03-C — Stand, less motion
Same identity and grid as 03-A. She stands, one hand on a rod, hair down, looking out of frame. Ink-smoke quieter, one ribbon across the mid-ground. Amber key lower. 50mm, more stillness. Fashion still inside the cage.

### 03-D — Body fragment
Editorial photograph, vertical 4:5. Crop: white-stockinged legs and the hip of the bodysuit, gold rods crossing, a slab of black ink. No face. Shallow DOF, amber bounce, grain. Architecture + fragment — wall-ready.

### 03-E — Face and one rod
Tight 4:5, 85mm. Face and shoulder, one gold rod cutting the frame, smoke over the far shoulder. White collar of the bodysuit. Warm under-light, Rembrandt. Hold identity from 03-A. No makeup-ad gloss.

### 03-F — Empty grid (void cousin)
Same gold lattice, same amber uplight and black ink, **no figure**. The scaffold is the subject. Vertical 2:3, receding vanishing point, haze. Fine grain. Use when the house needs a field, not a model.

### 03-G — Cooler brass, night
Same leap as 03-A but grade cooler: brass not fire, smoke more charcoal than ink, less orange. White suit still the coldest thing. Hold pose and identity. Iterate axis: grade only.

### 03-H — Small in the infinite
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, 35mm. Same woman, same suit, but she is small in the lower third, the lattice enormous. Ink weather in the distance. Sense of scale like a hall, not a set. Grain. Solitary — never a second body.
