# FRAMED-MOCK — wall-ready from a keeper

**When to use:** The still is already a keeper (or a chosen iterate). You are making the *room*, not a new artwork. Match Drop 01’s lifestyle mocks: charcoal wall, thin black frame, marble, velvet, one dried branch, quiet light.

Do **not** invent a new scene inside the frame. The frame contains `{KEEPER}` as-is.

Grounded in the six Drop 01 files in `images/` — every public piece is already a framed mock of this family.

---

## How to mock

1. Start from the keeper file. Attach it.  
2. The **artwork inside the frame** is that image, uncropped if possible (or a 2:3/4:5 crop you already approved).  
3. Pick **one** room recipe below.  
4. Generate **2–3** mocks max. Pick the one where the print is readable and the furniture does not steal.  
5. No Printful create. No SKU. This is site + inquire language only.

### House room lock

| Piece | Lock |
|-------|------|
| **Wall** | Matte charcoal or near-black, slight tooth. Not white gallery, not brick, not wallpaper. |
| **Frame** | Thin matte black wood or metal. Full-bleed print (no mat) *or* a very thin off-white reveal — Drop 01 uses both; default **no mat**. Deep enough to catch a small shadow. |
| **Glass** | Museum glass: soft reflection of a window mullion or a picture light, never a mirror of the photographer, never a readable room in the glass. |
| **Scale** | Large vertical. The print is the biggest object on the wall. Gallery height: center ~60" / 150 cm. Console sits below, not overlapping. |
| **Furniture** | Black marble (white vein) console or cylinder side table, thin brass or gold legs. Optional: black velvet armchair edge, gold piping. |
| **Props** | Stack of 2–3 black books (no readable real titles). Matte black ceramic vessel, dried dark branches. Nothing cute, nothing logo. |
| **Room light** | Dim. Either a soft window-mullion shadow across the wall, or a single warm picture light / spot on the frame. The art is the brightest thing. |
| **Refuse** | White IKEA rooms, oak floors as hero, plants in grow-pots, gallery labels with lorem, extra frames fighting, color-of-the-week walls, shop lighting, people in the room. |

### M-07 — Cooler stone

### M-08 — Scale check (human-absent)
The room of M-01, pulled back enough to read true scale: large vertical print, console width, chair height. No person. If the print looks like a postage stamp or a mural, reject and rerun. `{KEEPER}` must be legible at this distance.

- If the **art inside** mutated: throw the mock away; do not iterate the mutation.

- Commerce: **parked**. Do not create a Printful product from this mock.
