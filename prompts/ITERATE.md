# ITERATE — keepers only

**When to use:** A human already said *this one*. You are not generating a new world. You are moving one axis and holding the rest.

FRAIMOUS rule: **iterate winners, do not batch-gen past a shortlist.** If you do not have a keeper path, stop and go back to `library/review.html`.

Attach the keeper (or describe it as `{KEEPER}`) and paste **one** prompt below. One axis per run. If two things change, you will not know what worked.

---

## How to iterate

1. Name the keeper (file + title).  
2. Pick **one** axis.  
3. Paste the matching prompt. Say what must not move.  
4. Generate **3** variants max. Pick one. Repeat if needed.  
5. Do not upscale until the iterate is the one.

### Variation axes

| Axis | What moves | What holds |
|------|------------|------------|
| **Crop** | 2:3 → 4:5, tighter face, wider set, worm’s-eye, fragment | Identity, wardrobe, set, grade |
| **Wardrobe** | Robe ↔ flight suit ↔ white technical ↔ gold western | Face, hair, pose structure, room |
| **Time of day** | Golden hour ↔ cabin night ↔ moonlight ↔ amber under-light | Identity, set geometry |
| **Grade** | Warmer / cooler / more grain / less AI sheen / more LDN Grey | Composition, wardrobe |
| **Hold identity** | Same face, bob/bun, body — new beat in the *same* room | Everything that makes it her |

House finish on every iterate: analog grain, no plastic skin, no new logos, no extra fingers.

## Ready-to-paste iterate prompts

Replace `{KEEPER}` with the file name or a one-line description. Attach the image when the tool allows.

### I-01 — Less AI sheen
Edit `{KEEPER}`. Same composition, same person, same wardrobe, same set. Remove plastic skin and the over-sharpened digital crunch. Add fine 35mm film grain, slightly softer highlight roll-off, real fabric texture. Do not restyle. Do not add objects. Do not change the crop.

### I-02 — More grain, more print
Edit `{KEEPER}`. Hold identity and frame. Push analog: visible 5219-style grain, a hint of halation on the brightest edge, slightly deeper blacks. It should look like a darkroom print, not a screen. No heavy vignette, no Instagram filter.

### I-03 — Grade: more LNDN Grey
Edit `{KEEPER}`. Hold pose and identity. Cool the midtones a notch, keep the golds as metal not fire, crush the near-blacks toward charcoal. Sparse, expensive, less amber candy. No teal-orange grade.

### I-04 — Grade: warmer window
Edit `{KEEPER}`. Hold everything. Shift key toward golden-hour window light: ochre bloom, longer warm shadows, slightly less silver in the metals. Do not turn it into sunset stock.

### I-05 — Crop tighter (4:5)
Re-frame `{KEEPER}` as a vertical 4:5. Same person, same clothes, same room. Move in: face + talisman (cup, bow, rifle, patch) fill the frame. Background becomes suggestion. Do not change wardrobe. Do not add a smile.

### I-06 — Crop wider (show the room)
Re-frame `{KEEPER}` as a vertical 2:3 with more set. Same identity. Let the porthole / lattice / hall / trees read as the other actor. She can be smaller. Do not add a second figure. Do not change clothes.

### I-07 — Time: cabin night
Edit `{KEEPER}`. Same identity, same seat, same wardrobe. Kill the golden hour. One practical key (switch panel, small lamp, moonlight through glass). Blue-black void in the window if there is one. Deeper grain. Hold the patch and the cup if they exist.

### I-08 — Time: last sun
Edit `{KEEPER}`. Same identity and blocking. Push the key to last-sun side light: brim or cheekbone hard, long shadow, warm metal. If there is a window, the outside goes ochre or amber. Do not add lensflare.

### I-09 — Wardrobe swap, hold her
Edit `{KEEPER}`. **Hold the face, hair, body, pose, and room.** Change only clothes to: [cream canvas flight suit with quiet 7 / dark satin robe with AMERICAN eagle patch / white high-neck bodysuit / olive jumpsuit / gold metallic western bodice + felt hat]. One of those, named. No new props except what the new clothes require.

### I-10 — Identity lock, new beat
Edit `{KEEPER}`. This is the same woman in the same world, one beat later: she has set the cup down / lowered the bow an inch / turned a page / stopped climbing / taken the hat off. Micro change. Do not relocate. Do not change grade.

### I-11 — Fragment
Edit `{KEEPER}`. Crop to a body fragment or object fragment that could hang alone: hands + cup, legs + gold rods, engraving + rose, bun + bow limb. No full face unless it is already a portrait. Hold textures and grade.

### I-12 — Gallery-wall light (pre-mock)
Edit `{KEEPER}`. Do not put it in a room yet. Recolor the *photograph* as if it will be seen under a warm picture light: slightly richer shadows, highlight that will read at 24 inches, no crushed blacks, no neon. Hold composition. Next step is `FRAMED-MOCK.md`, not more gens.

## Do not

- Run all twelve on one keeper in one sitting.  
- Iterate a 2/5. Kill it.  
- Change face *and* clothes *and* room. That is a new gen — use a PACK.  
- Add watermarks, signatures, or FRAIMOUS type into the image.  
- Upscale until Shea points at one file.
