# PACK-02 — Western Talisman

**Lane:** object / talisman · western editorial  
**When to use:** Ceremonial West, not costume West. A wide felt hat, an engraved long gun treated as jewelry, flowers where the barrel should speak, gold metallic against night trees, a field of stardust. This is *Floral Rifle Cowgirl* and the inbox twins (`frm_045`, `frm_024`). Open when you want a wall piece that is half fashion, half relic.

Grounded in: `images/06_floral_rifle.jpg`.

---

## Locked style bible

| Axis | Lock |
|------|------|
| **Light** | Warm golden-hour *side* key, as if last sun through trees. Deep modeled shadows under the brim. Speculars on engraved metal and sequin/jacquard — small, not disco. Night ambient is charcoal, not navy neon. |
| **Lens** | 50mm or 85mm, f/1.8–2.8. Vertical 2:3 (hero) or 4:5 (tighter relic). Medium or waist-up. Background: soft woodland bokeh, no horizon postcard. |
| **Palette** | Chocolate, taupe, bronze, antique gold, charcoal. Accents: dusty pink rose, cream daisy, cool blued steel. Grade: rich, warm, filmic. Stardust is *fine cross-shaped glints*, not glitter dump. |
| **Wardrobe / set** | High-quality brown felt cowboy hat (not straw, not costume stitch). Sleek dark bob. Metallic gold / bronze jacquard or sequin bodice — editorial, not festival. Ornate engraved shotgun or rifle: polished walnut, gold filigree, blued or chrome barrel. Fresh roses + daisies growing *from* barrel and receiver as if the gun were a vase. Outdoor night, trees, no town, no horses unless tiny and out of focus. |
| **Refuse** | Aggression, aiming at camera, muzzle flash, blood, tactical black rifles, AR platforms, red-dot optics, American-flag kitsch, denim-on-denim catalog cowgirl, neon, plastic flowers, heavy CGI glitter, desert-noon bleach, smile, celebrity. |

**Tone:** ceremonial, not violent. The gun is a talisman. The flowers win.

## Ready-to-paste prompts

### 02-A — Floral rifle, gold bodice, night trees (hero)
Editorial fashion photograph, vertical 2:3, 50mm f/2. A woman with a sleek dark bob and a wide-brim brown felt cowboy hat, waist-up, holding an ornate engraved shotgun vertically in front of her. Pink roses and white daisies grow from the barrel and cluster at the walnut stock. Shimmering metallic gold bikini-bodice / jacquard, warm bronzed skin. Outdoor night, soft-focus trees. Warm directional side light, deep brim shadow. Fine analog grain. A restrained field of tiny star-like glints in the air — stardust, not craft glitter. Poised, not aggressive. FRAIMOUS still.

### 02-B — Diagonal rifle, three-quarter, less sparkle
Same identity as 02-A. Medium close-up, rifle held diagonally across the torso, flowers erupting from the receiver. Three-quarter face, editorial neutral, looking just off camera. Hat brim cuts a hard shadow. Background woodland bokeh, golden-hour side key. Reduce the stardust to a few speculars on metal and petals. More grain, less magic overlay. Tactile felt, cold engraving, soft rose.

### 02-C — Relic still (no figure)
Editorial object still, vertical 4:5, 85mm. The ornate rifle alone, laid or standing in dark — walnut, gold filigree, blued barrel. Roses and daisies grow from the ejection port and muzzle as if the steel were earth. One warm side light, long shadow. Fine grain, black void around. No hands, no hat, no logo. Talisman.

### 02-D — Hat-brim night, full figure
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, 35mm. Full figure in a dark meadow edge, trees behind. Same hat, gold metallic top, dark trousers or bare midriff kept editorial. Rifle used as a vertical staff, flowers at the muzzle catching the last light. Face half in brim shadow. Sparse stardust. Fine grain. Solitary, ceremonial.

### 02-E — Engraving close
Macro-editorial, vertical 4:5. Fill the frame with gold scrollwork on the receiver, a rose stem threaded through the metal, one daisy against blued steel. Shallow DOF, warm side specular, analog grain. No face. Jewelry scale.

### 02-F — Hat off, moonlight
Same woman, hat in her off hand against the hip. Dark bob only. Rifle horizontal, flowers quieter (two roses). Cooler moonlight key from camera-left, warm practical nowhere. Night trees. Grain up, stardust almost gone. Hold identity.

### 02-G — Crossed relics
Still life, vertical 2:3. Two ceremonial rifles crossed on dark wood or black velvet: one blooming heavily, one bare engraved metal. A single fallen daisy. Warm rake light. No figure. Collector object, not armory.

### 02-H — Crossover: hat + cream flight suit
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3. Same hat and dark bob, but wardrobe is the cream canvas flight suit from Capsule Domestic (no patch war). She holds the floral rifle loosely, not aimed. Night trees, warm side light, fine grain, almost no glitter. House crossover — only use if iterating a keeper that already mixes lanes.
