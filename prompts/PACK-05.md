# PACK-05 — Void Field

**Lane:** landscape / void · architecture shadow · object still  
**When to use:** When the house needs a wall that is not a woman-with-prop. Ochre desert through glass, a starfield in a pill window, an empty gold grid, a lunar path with classical ruins, a cup left on the table. Drawn from the *windows* of Drop 01 and inbox `frm_038` (lunar columns + pods), `frm_022` (black-void object) — not from generic space art.

Grounded in: the portholes of `02`, `04`, `05`; empty-set cousins of `01` and `03`.

---

## Locked style bible

| Axis | Lock |
|------|------|
| **Light** | Either: (a) raking golden hour through glass, haze bloom; or (b) hard lunar side light, long black shadows; or (c) object in a true black field with one warm rake. Pick one. |
| **Lens** | 35mm for fields and ruins; 50–85mm for window-sill stills. Vertical 2:3 or 4:5. Deep space is *quiet* — pin stars, not nebula posters. |
| **Palette** | Ochre / pale sun / haze; or cool grey stone + white satin leftover + black sky; or gold rods + ink; or cream ceramic + charcoal. Never Hubble pink-cyan. |
| **Wardrobe / set** | Prefer **no figure**. If a figure appears she is small, back-to, or a fragment. Sets: capsule sill + porthole; infinite gold lattice empty; carved hall empty; lunar grey ground, fluted columns, circular ribbon-window pods, crescent, star void; white cup / broadsheet / figurine as the only subject. |
| **Refuse** | Earth-from-orbit cliché, Milky Way phone-wallpaper, rocket launch, astronaut visor selfie, nebula candy, matte-painting tourism, readable signage, people walking toward camera, blue marble. |

**Rule:** if you can hang it next to Drop 01 and it still looks like the same house, keep it. If it looks like a stock “space” canvas, kill it.

## Ready-to-paste prompts

### 05-A — Desert through glass
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, 50mm. No figure. Inside a capsule: warm wood sill, one white ceramic cup at the edge of frame, a rounded-rectangular porthole filling most of the image. Through the glass: hazy ochre-orange desert planet, pale low sun, atmospheric bloom, no buildings. Soft window light, analog grain, 1970s still. Quiet.

### 05-B — Star void, empty table
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3. Pill-shaped porthole of crisp white stars on deep black. Empty white pedestal table, folded broadsheet, cup and saucer, thin steam. Analog switch row above the glass. Hard side light, shadow of the cup on metal. No figure. Fine grain.

### 05-C — Empty gold lattice
Vertical 2:3. Three-dimensional scaffold of thin gold rods receding to a vanishing point, thick black ink-smoke, amber uplight, ochre haze. No figure. Same world as Golden Grid Climber with the body removed. Analog grain, not Tron.

### 05-D — Lunar path, columns, pods
Editorial photograph, vertical 2:3, 35mm. Desolate grey cratered ground, a winding pale stone path. Weathered fluted classical columns holding a circular multi-level pod with horizontal ribbon windows; a second brutalist pod among dark rocks. Pitch-black star sky, a realistic crescent high right. Hard directional light, long shadows. Optional: a tiny distant figure in white satin, back to us — or none. Fine grain, LNDN Grey monochrome luxury. No Earth postcard.

### 05-E — Gilded hall, mullion shadow
Architecture still, vertical 2:3. Carved pillar, gold leaf in the capitals, tall window throwing a mullion cross on dark stone. Dust in the beam. Empty. Low-key, analog grain. The Archer hall without Archer.

### 05-F — Cup, steam, wood
Object still, vertical 4:5, 85mm. White ceramic mug, quiet flag decal (no type lockup), visible steam, warm wood planks, a strip of starfield bokeh at the top. Side light, long shadow. Talisman. No hands.

### 05-G — Figurine on carved desk
Object still, vertical 4:5. Ornate dark-wood desk, small ceramic Shiba, empty cream cup, porthole of ochre haze behind, out of focus. Window light. No figure. Companion to Desert Sunrise Coffee.

### 05-H — Black-field relic
Editorial object on true black, vertical 4:5. One ceremonial object only — the floral rifle *or* the white cup *or* the wood recurve — lit by a single warm rake, fine grain, a few star-like speculars. No environment. Use sparingly; do not start a wireframe-hologram subplot unless Shea asks.
